New logo and new website

2023-10-25

As you may have noticed we have modernized our company image with a brand new logo. This move is to detach us from the political definition of the word "radical" that was given bad press in the recent years and that has no relation with our way of doing business. We want to rather emphasize on the many positive definitions of the term RADICAL that are aligned with us.
